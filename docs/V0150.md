# 0.15.0 · Account capital policy / 帳戶資金與風險額度

## Use it / 操作方式

Open **Accounts → Account settings → Account capital and risk limits**. Each account has its own editable limits. “Signal evaluation style” at the top of the market page now affects signals only; it no longer changes account funds or default addition counts.

前往 **帳戶與倉位 → 帳戶設定 → 帳戶資金與風險額度**。每個帳戶獨立設定，舊帳戶會保留升級前實際生效的額度。頁首「信號評估風格」只影響交易機會，不再控制帳戶資金。

| Limit / 限額 | Meaning / 計算口徑 |
|---|---|
| Margin / 保證金使用率 | Total occupied position margin / native wallet risk equity; new order costs must also fit available funds. / 已佔用保證金占本錢包風控權益；新增訂單另須有足夠可用資金支付費用。 |
| Gross exposure / 總名義敞口 | Sum of absolute position notionals, expressed as a multiple of equity. It is not margin and does not net long and short exposure. / 持倉名義價值絕對值合計，以權益倍數表示，不是保證金，也不以多空互抵。 |
| Per action stop budget / 每次止損預算 | Incremental opening or addition loss at the stop, including estimated fees/slippage. / 本次開倉或加倉的預計止損損失，包含估算費用與滑點。 |
| Portfolio stop budget / 組合止損預算 | Existing estimated stop losses plus the proposed action, in native settlement currency. / 現有倉位加新增動作的止損損失合計，使用原生結算幣種。 |
| Daily loss / 當日虧損 | Equity loss from the first verified UTC-day valuation; paper baseline is captured without requiring an order. Deposits/withdrawals are not performance-adjusted. / 相對 UTC 當日首次有效估值的權益損失；模擬盤不用等下單才記錄。充值及提領未作績效調整。 |
| Asset group / 同類資產 | Crypto and equity groups each have a gross-exposure / equity cap. / 加密資產及股票類資產分組計算名義敞口上限。 |
| Liquidation buffers / 強平緩衝 | Independently configurable stop-to-liquidation and mark-to-liquidation distances, as percent of price. / 止損至強平及現價至強平的額外最小距離，分別設定。 |

Disabled optional limits are excluded, with no default fallback. Available funds, permissions, fresh quotes, contract size/precision and a stop before liquidation remain mandatory. Different settlement wallets are never combined. Exchange risk equity uses the lower of reported equity and verified available funds plus known position margin; the UI shows this conservative basis separately. Paper/projected liquidation still uses the documented approximation, not verified venue maintenance tiers.

關閉某項即不納入該項檢查，不恢復預設值。實際可用資金、交易權限、有效行情、合約精度及止損先於強平等基礎檢查仍保留。不同結算錢包不混用。交易所風控權益採回報權益與「可用資金＋已知倉位保證金」兩者較低值，明細會顯示這個保守口徑。強平估算並非交易所精確檔位認證。

## Sizing and migration / 倉位分配與遷移

- Fixed quantity is either accepted in full or rejected with a breakdown of needed, used and allowed amounts. Maximum/system sizing can shrink to fit the same account budgets.
- Planned additions reserve room and share the same account limits and addition count. Profit or loss does not create an exception to capital checks.
- Conservative/balanced/aggressive presets fill visible editable fields. Changing a preset is not a promise of a suitable trade size or profit. AI preferences guide evaluation within explicit authority.
- Old global exposure/risk coefficients, group fraction and portfolio multiple are copied into each account once. The original configuration is privately retained under `v2_risk_pre_v015` and in the deployment backup.
- Previously hidden system-sizing discounts become explicit per-pair allocation values. Expand “查看 / 调整此交易对资金分配”; blank optional fields inherit the account limit, populated fields apply the stricter bound. The legacy `system_level` field is compatibility metadata only and has no execution effect. No global risk-profile multiplier or daily-loss ×5 formula remains in order sizing.
- Strategy exports containing the retired group/portfolio controls remain readable; those controls no longer appear in the strategy editor or AI optimizer and cannot override account settings.

固定數量不足時不偷偷縮量；最大／系統倉位可縮至允許數量。舊有隱含折扣已轉成交易對下方可見、可編輯的分配數值；選擇繼承帳戶額度時清空相應欄位。既有策略檔案仍可讀取，但不能再藉由策略修改帳戶資金上限。遷移不自動提高舊帳戶授權，組合風險現在明確計入估算成本。

## AI capital management / AI 帳戶額度管理

1. Connect AI and select a model in Data & AI.
2. In the account settings, select a capital preference and explicitly authorize editable limits, numeric lower/upper bounds and optional switch changes. No parameters are delegated by default.
3. Save settings. **Evaluate and apply** works with the automatic switch off. The automatic switch enables reevaluation after verified fills, closes, additions, configuration changes, material equity changes and review expiry.
4. Confirmed trades must have synchronized funds/positions. Minimum review interval defaults to 60 seconds (30–3600), validity to 1800 seconds (60–86400), and material equity change to 2% (0.1–50%). Requests are coalesced; one capital-review worker runs at a time.
5. While a required assessment is missing, stale or invalidated by a fill, entries/additions wait. Existing protective exits keep running. A failed refresh does not discard an otherwise still-valid policy assessment.

先連接 AI，再逐項設定可修改範圍並儲存。手動「評估並應用」可在自動開關關閉時使用；開啟自動後，確認成交及資金同步才觸發。連續變動合併處理，避免每個行情事件都呼叫 AI。等待有效評估時暫停新增風險，既有止盈止損繼續。此功能會傳送匿名化帳戶資金、倉位及行情至目前的 AI 服務並消耗額度。

The AI receives current budgets, native balance/available funds, positions, estimated fees and liquidation, available maintenance data, opportunities and explicitly configured pair allocations. Exchange reads include regular and conditional open orders across symbols. An unverified/non-reduce-only pending order, a full page that may be truncated, unmanaged positions or incomplete funds blocks new risk; the system does not assume the omitted amount is zero. Such cases need reconciliation, not invented account data.

OKX algo orders are queried by their required order types, including stop, OCO, trailing, iceberg, TWAP and chase orders, following the [official pending algo-order API](https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/#order-book-trading-algo-trading-get-algo-order-list).

AI cannot edit its own authority, pair permissions, leverage, margin mode, live execution gates or certification. Unauthorized, duplicate, out-of-range, malformed, incomplete and stale results are rejected atomically. Settings changed while a review is running cannot be overwritten. Quote-only exchange mark updates do not masquerade as fills; actual wallet/position changes invalidate prior approval.

AI 不能更改自己的權限、交易對授權、槓桿、保證金模式或實盤開關。越界、不完整、重複或過期結果不生效。資料不足時明確顯示原因；未核驗掛單與外部倉位不當成零占用，也不自動接管。

## Existing positions and records / 現有倉位與紀錄

New policy applies immediately to new risk. When enabled, the existing position reviewer is queued after policy changes and receives the current account limits. It may propose existing authorized protection/reduction actions. A policy edit alone does not force liquidation or rewrite an existing exit trigger. The mark-to-liquidation protection buffer is stored with a new position (or confirmed addition); prior positions retain their protection. Stops are not loosened to make a budget fit.

每次實際修改會在同一筆交易中備份舊額度，記錄新舊數值、來源與原因，保留每帳戶最近 100 次。透過「額度明細／調整紀錄」檢視、匯出，或將舊額度載入設定表單，核對後儲存。載入不會自動改動 AI 授權、交易對設定或直接下單；自動 AI 開啟時，儲存後仍可按既有授權再次評估。

Only actual AI policy changes emit the AI intervention notification category, honoring independent Telegram and sound switches. An unchanged assessment updates its status without sending a repeated intervention alert. Paper tests and exchange mocks do not certify live execution; live execution remains off by default.
